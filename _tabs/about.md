## Hi, I'm Vieet

I'm a **blockchain security researcher** focused on protocol-level correctness, consensus clients, and the messy edges where distributed systems meet real money.

My work spans reading and auditing critical infrastructure — from validator clients and ledger software to DeFi primitives — and writing up what I find so others can learn from it.

### Focus areas

- Consensus & client security (e.g. Firedancer, Ethereum clients)
- Ledger / L1 protocol review (e.g. XRPL / `rippled`)
- Smart contract and DeFi vulnerability research
- Formal reasoning about state machines, invariants, and failure modes

### This site

Notes, audit write-ups, and research posts. Expect deep dives into protocol bugs, threat models, and practical review methodology — not just CVE headlines.
